\#####################Root 、Twrp and Xposed #######################

trun your phone to "fastboot mode" and connect to PC

move all flie in adb to "C:\"

![img](http://www.kulafan.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/explorer_2018-01-26_12-57-02-300x227.png)

use "Win+R" type "Cmd" and enter

and type

***cd c:\
fastboot devices***

to make sure your devices is connected

![img](http://www.kulafan.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/cmd_2018-01-26_13-02-13.png)

type

***fastboot oem unlock\***   # unlock your phone
***fastboot boot recovery-3.1.1-1.img***      #flash twrp to your phone

wait for your devices ,you will enter Twrp

put "SR5-SuperSU-v2.82-SR5-20171001224502.zip" to phone

and Install via Twrp

Root is done
